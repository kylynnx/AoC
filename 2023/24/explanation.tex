    Let us denote the coordinates of an arbitrary hailstone $i$ by $x^i$, $y^i$, and $z^i$, further let $v_x^i$,
    $v_y^i$, and $v_z^i$ be the respective velocity.
    Coordinates with no index $i$ will denote the coordinates of the stone, we need to throw.
    Then, the following equations need to be fulfilled for the stone to hit the hailstone
    \begin{eqnarray*}
        x^i + t v_x^i & = & x + t v_x \\
        y^i + t v_y^i & = & y + t v_y \\
        z^i + t v_z^i & = & z + t v_z.
    \end{eqnarray*}

    Solving for the time, leaves us with
    \begin{eqnarray*}
        t & = & \left(x - x^i\right) \left(v_x^i - v_x\right)^{-1} \\
        t & = & \left(y - y^i\right) \left(v_y^i - v_y\right)^{-1} \\
        t & = & \left(z - z^i\right) \left(v_z^i - v_z\right)^{-1}.
    \end{eqnarray*}

    Equating two of these and simple manipulations, will end up as
    \begin{equation*}
        y v_x - x v_y = -x v_y^i + y v_x^i + y^i v_x - x^i v_y + x^i v_y^i - y^i v_x^i.
    \end{equation*}

    The LHS is independent of $t$ and the index $i$.
    Repeating the same procedure for another arbitrary $k \neq i$, and equating, leaves
    \begin{equation*}
        \left(v_y^i - v_y^k\right) x + \left(v_x^k - v_x^i\right) y + \left(y^k - y^i\right) v_x + \left(x^i - x^k\right) v_y
        = x^i v_y^i - x^k v_y^k + y^k v_x^k - y^i v_x^i
    \end{equation*}

    This is one equation with six unknowns.
    Taking into account the two other combinations of coordinates $\left(\left(x, z\right), \left(y, z\right)\right)$
    produces three equations with three unknowns.
    Last step is to find another hailstone $l \neq i,k$ gives us three more equations.
    Now solve using your favorite solver.
